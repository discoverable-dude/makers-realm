# Unit prices

Rule: variants whose Size, Type, Pack Size or Capacity value is a plain quantity in ml, L, g or kg get a unit price. Liquids show per litre, solids per kg. Lengths and widths (mm, m) are not included. 577 variants across 142 products qualify.

## Trial, 20 Nov 2026
Set on three products only: Liquid Latex for Make Up Effects, Fine Casting Plaster, Hato Design Acrylic Paint- Chromakey (8 variants). Read back from the store: correct. Before this none of the variants had a unit price (all empty), so undo means clearing the unit price measurement and unticking "Show unit price" on those variants.

The other 139 products have not been changed.

## Outcome, 20 Nov 2026
Not wanted for now. "Show unit price" was switched off again on the three trial products, so nothing shows on the site. The unit price measurement values are still stored on those 8 variants but are hidden. The other 139 products were never changed. Added to the client approval sheet as a question in case the client wants it.
